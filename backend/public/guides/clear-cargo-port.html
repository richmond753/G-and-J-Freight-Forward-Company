Customs

How to clear cargo at the port

A plain-language overview of clearing imports through Tema. Exact steps can vary by cargo type and regime — your forwarder and licensed Customs agent handle the filings.

1. Gather the core documents

  • Bill of Lading (or Air Waybill)
  • Commercial invoice
  • Packing list
  • Import declaration / permits where required
  • Certificate of origin or other certificates if applicable

Upload these early via our document vault so operations can pre-check gaps.

2. Classification and valuation

Goods are classified with HS codes and valued for duty. Wrong codes or undervaluation cause delays and penalties. See our HS code basics guide.

3. Duties, taxes, and fees

Expect import duty, VAT, and possible levies depending on the product. Your agent calculates payable amounts after assessment.

4. Examination and release

Some consignments are selected for examination. After payment and clearance, the terminal releases the cargo for haulage to your warehouse or site.

5. Inland delivery

Once released, road haulage moves the container or loose cargo. Track driver updates on our haulage status page.
