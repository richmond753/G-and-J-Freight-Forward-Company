Ocean freight

FCL vs LCL

Choosing between a full container and shared space is mostly about volume, urgency, and how much handling you can accept.

FCL — Full Container Load

You book an entire 20ft or 40ft container. Cargo is sealed at origin and typically opened only at destination (or under Customs control).

  • Best when you have enough volume (roughly enough to fill most of a container)
  • Usually faster door-to-door and fewer handlings
  • Rate is per container, not per cubic metre

LCL — Less than Container Load

Your cargo shares a container with other shippers. A consolidator stuffs and strips the box at CFS facilities.

  • Best for smaller volumes
  • You pay mainly by volume (CBM) and/or weight
  • More handling points — plan packing and lead time carefully

Quick decision tips

  • Nearly filling a 20ft? Compare FCL vs LCL total landed cost — FCL often wins.
  • Urgent, fragile, or high-value? FCL reduces touch points.
  • Irregular small shipments? LCL or air may fit better.
